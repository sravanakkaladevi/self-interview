    FAQ

    Questions shop owners ask.

    Do you promise sales?

    No. Anyone promising guaranteed sales is guessing. We promise disciplined creative work, local targeting, WhatsApp-ready offers, and honest monthly reports on views, saves, followers, and enquiries.

    Who pays for ads?

    You pay Meta directly from your own ad account so you always keep 100% control of your spend and data. We recommend a realistic local budget and manage the targeting, creatives, and optimization.

    How does the free trial work?

    Before starting, we agree on exact deliverables for 14 days and 2–3 numbers to track together. We execute the work first at ₹0 agency fee. After two weeks we meet to review the numbers — you pay only if you want to continue on a monthly plan.

    How long to see results?

    Reach, saves, and WhatsApp enquiries from local Meta ads typically begin within the first 7–14 days. Organic reel momentum and follower trust compound over 30–90 days of consistent posting.

    Never. We ask to be added to your page as an official partner in Meta Business Suite so your account always stays under your own login.
